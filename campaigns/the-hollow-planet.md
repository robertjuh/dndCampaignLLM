**Setting**

```text
Kharox is a remote, mysterious planet with a nearly barren, rocky, inhospitable surface. Beneath it stretches an enormous interconnected world of caves, tunnels, underground lakes and rivers, towering crystal formations, ancient ruins, and vast subterranean chambers.

Glowing green Energy Crystals hold extraordinary magical power. Explorers seek them to power machinery, enchant equipment, build settlements, and activate ancient technology. Richer deposits lie deeper underground, where the dangers also grow.

Rock caverns connect with volcanic depths, frozen chambers, rare crystal caves, and the abandoned structures of an unknown civilization. Living Rock Monsters and their lava, ice, and crystal-infused relatives inhabit this frontier alongside other creatures adapted to darkness. Their needs and temperaments vary; encountering a creature does not automatically mean combat.

This is an adventurous fantasy world inspired by the classic LEGO Rock Raiders PC game, combining underground exploration, mining, survival, dungeon crawling, and mystery.
```


**The premise**

```text
The party begins at a small underground expedition outpost on Kharox. It is a refuge for resting, trading, crafting, storing discoveries, and preparing expeditions into the surrounding caverns. The unexplored tunnels beyond it contain valuable resources, strange inhabitants, and evidence of a forgotten civilization.

Each expedition can uncover new routes, crystal deposits, hidden chambers, ancient machinery, or people who need help or pursue interests of their own. Excavation can open passages and shortcuts, while hazards and the needs of underground creatures complicate the search for resources. Returning with discoveries can help the outpost grow and support further exploration.

The deeper the explorers venture, the greater the potential rewards and dangers. Strange crystal formations and geological phenomena suggest that Kharox holds secrets beyond its mineral wealth. Let those mysteries emerge through discoveries and player decisions. The party chooses its own ambitions, allies, destinations, and risks; there is no predetermined quest sequence or required ending.
```
