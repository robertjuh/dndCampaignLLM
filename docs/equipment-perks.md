# Protective equipment perks

New Uncommon or higher armour, helmets, boots and shields roll one saved passive
perk from their eligible pool. Existing items keep their saved mechanics. New
Common protective pieces instead roll one +1 attribute and one -1 attribute;
matching attributes cancel, including in the displayed bonuses. Attributes are
applied only while equipped and never satisfy equipment requirements.

Perk strengths are 1 / 2 / 3 for Uncommon / Rare or Cursed / Legendary. Hardy and
Fortified use twice those values. Up to four distinct equipped copies add their
strengths together; the strongest four count, and a two-handed item counts once.
Round-limited triggers use that combined strength once, rather than multiplying
the number of activations. Backpack items grant no passive effects.

| Perk | Eligible types | Effect of combined strength N |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reinforced | Armour, helmet, shield | First enemy hit each round: prevent N damage after shields/barriers, before Shocked. |
| Thorns | Armour, shield | First enemy hit each round that costs HP: deal N damage back. |
| Bulwark | Armour, shield | Defend grants N extra Defense until the next turn. |
| Vigilant | Helmet, boots | +N initiative. |
| Escape Artist | Helmet, boots | +N to fleeing checks. |
| Recuperating | Armour, helmet, boots | Recover N extra HP after a successful encounter. |
| Scrambler | Boots, shield | Failed fleeing check: +N Defense until the next turn. |
| Quickstep | Helmet, boots | Successful main-action movement: +N Defense until the next turn. |
| Enduring | Armour, helmet, boots, shield | Prevent N total ongoing ailment damage per turn, including Shocked. Other effects and durations remain. |
| Rescuer | Helmet, shield | Basic Help Up restores N additional HP. |
| Restorative | Armour, helmet | First potion/ability heal received each combat round restores N extra HP. |
| Opening Strike | Helmet, boots | +N to the first damaging attack roll per encounter; a miss spends it. |
| Retaliator | Armour, shield | After losing HP to an enemy hit, next successful damaging attack gains N damage; hits refresh without multiplying it. |
| Opportunist | Helmet, boots | Once per round, +N damage on a hit against an already afflicted enemy. |
| Last Stand | Armour, shield | +N Defense while conscious and below half maximum HP. |
| Fortified | Armour, helmet, shield | Prevent N of the extra critical-hit dice damage, retaining ordinary-hit damage. |
| Hardy | Armour, helmet, boots | +N maximum HP; equipping does not heal and removal can clamp HP. |

Existing attribute bonuses, immunities, shield blocking and Legendary abilities
continue to apply. Combat effects are saved in engine receipts and narrated from
those results. Encounter flags survive saved rounds and clear on victory/escape;
temporary Defense expires at the next initiative turn even when incapacitated.

Item bonus icons open native popover tooltips by click, touch, Enter or Space.
Escape or clicking outside dismisses them. Starting equipment selection and bonus
inspection have separate controls.
